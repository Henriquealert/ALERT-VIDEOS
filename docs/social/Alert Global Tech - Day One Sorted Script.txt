denser voiceover, an on-screen line on every beat, a 30 s cut with its own script).

Main cut: 60 s, 24 fps. 16x9 (1920x1080) and 9x16 (1080x1920, 250 px kept clear top and bottom).
Feed cut: 30 s, 24 fps, 4x5 (1080x1350).
Voiceover: ElevenLabs, voice "Ray D - Adult Male Energetic and Engaging American", model eleven_v4.
Music and sound design: original, synthesised for this film (scripts/make-day-one-audio.py).
Call to action: Request a Quote. URL: alertglobaltech.com

NOTES ON THE BUILD
- No numbers, prices, clients or dates are invented. All quantities and prices are [00].
- Changed from the original brief at Henrique's request (too slow, too few words): the voiceover
  is rewritten and longer, "Picture this." is replaced by the No laptop / No login lines, and
  the 30 s cut now shows the fix (the brief's beats 1, 3, 7, 8 left out the product).
- Fonts: only the supplied brand fonts (Archivo Narrow, DM Sans, Instrument Serif). Headlines that
  the brief set in Archivo ExtraBold use Archivo Narrow Bold caps; DM Mono tags use DM Sans caps.
- Colours: the four brand colours plus the blue gradient stops only. The "teal" desktop and
  "grey" dialogs in the brief are an Electric Blue to deep blue desktop with ivory dialogs.
- The Ink wordmark is made from the approved Ivory file's shape (see public/brand/README.md).
- The voice licence follows the ElevenLabs account plan. Confirm commercial use before publishing.
