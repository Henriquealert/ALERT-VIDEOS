ALERT GLOBAL TECH - DAY ONE, SORTED.
Brand film for startup founders. Script and shot list as built.

Main cut: 60 s, 24 fps. 16x9 (1920x1080) and 9x16 (1080x1920, 250 px kept clear top and bottom).
Feed cut: 30 s, 24 fps, 4x5 (1080x1350), beats 1, 3, 7 and 8.
Voiceover: ElevenLabs, voice "Everett - Confident American Ads", model eleven_v4.
Music and sound design: original, synthesised for this film (scripts/make-day-one-audio.py).
Call to action: Request a Quote. URL: alertglobaltech.com


MAIN CUT (60 s)

1. HOOK  0:00-0:04  Deep Ink
   Pixel cursors and pointing hands swarm in, each dragging in one letter, with a click on
   every landing. The headline holds, then crumbles into falling lime, blue and ivory blocks.
   On screen: NEW HIRE STARTS MONDAY (lime dot)
   VO: none
   Sound: lo-fi synth intro, a cursor click per letter.

2. PICTURE THIS  0:04-0:07  Deep Ink
   Ink frame, then a slow push-in on a flat desk in ivory and blue: empty chair pushed out,
   sealed laptop box, lime sticky note reading "laptop?".
   On screen: PICTURE THIS (lime dot), Archivo Narrow Bold Italic
   VO 0:04.25: "Your first hire starts Monday."

3. CHAOS  0:07-0:14  Retro desktop, Electric Blue to deep blue
   Old-style dialog windows (ivory with blue title bars, flickering lime edges) pop up faster
   and faster until they fill the screen. The last one lands centre, with a small screen shake.
   Dialogs: Who ordered the laptops? / Mac or Windows? / How many Microsoft 365 licences? /
   Is the Wi-Fi password still password? / Copilot or not? / Which vendor was that? /
   Are we cooked?
   VO 0:07.5: "Then the questions start."
   Sound: rising dialog chimes, faster blips as the windows multiply, an error bonk on the last.

4. EVERY TAB OPEN  0:14-0:17  Deep Ink
   Near-empty frame. A cursor clicks and drags a solid Electric Blue highlight across the
   second line.
   On screen: ONE FOUNDER. EVERY TAB OPEN (lime dot)
   Sound: the music pulls back under a low-pass; mouse-down and mouse-up clicks.

5. THE TANGLE  0:17-0:26  Deep Ink
   Thin blue rails and ivory crosshairs knot five floating blocks together: LAPTOPS, LICENCES,
   WI-FI, SECURITY, TRAINING. Tiny cursors wander along the rails, stop and turn back. The camera
   starts close on LICENCES and pulls back to show the whole knot.
   VO 0:18: "Laptops from one place. Licences from another. Wi-Fi from whoever picks up."
   Sound: full lo-fi build with glitch stutters and a riser, then a hard cut to silence at 0:26.

6. THE FIX  0:26-0:46  Soft Ivory
   Calm, spacious. Hairline cards with rounded corners slide in, slowly:
   - Message card, typed out: "We're hiring six people this quarter."
   - Quote card (ONE SUPPLIER): Dell or Apple laptops, Microsoft 365, Copilot, Ubiquiti Wi-Fi,
     Fortinet firewall. Every quantity, price and the total shown as [00].
   - Setup checklist, one lime tick at a time: Sourced through TD SYNNEX / Configured /
     Licences assigned / Certiport training booked
   - Small Deep Ink card: READY FOR MONDAY (lime dot)
   VO 0:27: "Alert sorts it in one go."
   VO 0:30: "One quote, one supplier."
   VO 0:35: "Set up, not just shipped. In English, Spanish or Portuguese."
   Sound: silence, then a warm, minimal pulse from 0:28; a soft tick on each checkbox.

7. RESOLUTION  0:46-0:52  Deep Ink
   The tangled lines snap straight into three ascending stepped blocks (lime, blue, outline).
   A chunky up-right arrow, blue to sky gradient, rises past them and exits top right.
   VO 0:47: "So you can get back to building."
   Sound: a snap on the lines, a soft swish under the arrow.

8. END CARD  0:52-1:00  Acid Lime
   A lime wipe sweeps left to right. Ink a!ert wordmark top-left (the only logo in the film).
   Headline: DAY ONE, sorted (ink dot). "DAY ONE," in caps, "sorted" in Instrument Serif Italic.
   CTA: ink pill, Request a Quote
   Below: alertglobaltech.com
   Small stepped blocks (ink, blue, outline) bottom-right.
   VO 0:54: "Hardware, software and AI, sorted."
   Sound: soft whoosh on the wipe; the pulse resolves, bell, fade to 1:00.

NOTES ON THE BUILD
- No numbers, prices, clients or dates are invented. All quantities and prices are [00].
- Fonts: only the supplied brand fonts are used (Archivo Narrow, DM Sans, Instrument Serif).
  Headlines that the brief set in Archivo ExtraBold use Archivo Narrow Bold caps; tags that the
  brief set in DM Mono use DM Sans caps.
- Colours: the four brand colours plus the blue gradient stops only. The "teal" desktop and
  "grey" dialogs in the brief are an Electric Blue to deep blue desktop with ivory dialogs.
- The Ink wordmark is made from the approved Ivory file's shape (see public/brand/README.md).
  Replace it with an official Ink file if one exists.
- The voice licence follows the ElevenLabs account plan. Confirm commercial use before publishing.
