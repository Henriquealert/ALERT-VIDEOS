# Alert Global Tech — Brand Guidelines 2026

The visual identity and brand book for Alert Global Tech, the US arm of Alert Informática. Built from *Brand Evolution 2026* (ALERT_BRANDING.pdf, Ana Chaguri). Revised 21 Oct 2026 after a page-by-page check against the deck; calls to action updated 27 Oct 2026 to match those in use. This is the standing reference for the website, LinkedIn, profile images, banners and email.

> A more striking identity is not the end goal. It is the tool for a more recognisable brand, clearer communication of our solutions, and more business opportunities. — Brand Evolution 2026 (translated)

## Content fundamentals

**Brand line.** Hardware, software and AI, sorted.
**Support line.** One supplier, from quote to setup.
**Platform.** "___, sorted." — Laptops, sorted. Networking, sorted. AI, made practical.

Voice (deck: social tone of voice): clear and direct; modern and confident; focused on real value for the business; educational and accessible; connected to the identity.

Rules that always apply:
- Describe what Alert does and the problem it solves. Never call customers "small and mid-sized businesses" or "SMBs".
- Name real products: Dell, HP, Lenovo, Apple, Cisco, Fortinet, Ubiquiti, Microsoft 365, Copilot, SharePoint. Sourced through TD SYNNEX; training through Certiport.
- Calls to action in title-style capitals, one per piece: Talk to an Expert, Request a Quote, Learn More, Book a Call, Book a Sprint.
- Short sentences. No hype words, no exclamation marks, no emoji.
- Never invent numbers, clients, testimonials or prices. The deck's "+40%", "300+ stores" and "20 employees, 20 laptops" are layout placeholders, not facts.
- Service in English, Spanish and Portuguese — say so as a concrete offer.

Content pillars (deck slide 17, adapted from Ramp): Corporate life, unfiltered ~30% · Tech, made simple ~30% · Business, upgraded ~25% · The Alert perspective ~15%. Lessons: content that entertains and educates; consistent, recognisable identity; short, provocative messages; focus on the customer's problem, not only the product; balance creativity and authority.

## Colour

| Token | Hex | Meaning (deck) |
|---|---|---|
| Electric Blue | #1644B8 | Trust, technology, credibility |
| Acid Lime | #E8FF58 | Energy, innovation, differentiation |
| Deep Ink | #101B32 | Structure, sophistication, focus |
| Soft Ivory | #F5F4EF | Balance, clarity, legibility |

Blue keeps the familiarity already tied to Alert; lime brings energy and contrast; ink and ivory balance the composition. Default ground is Deep Ink. Rotate grounds across a feed: ink, blue, ivory, with lime for announcements and statement pieces.

Contrast: ivory on ink 15.6 · lime on ink 15.4 · ivory on blue 7.5 · lime on blue 7.4 · blue on ivory 7.5. **Never** blue text on ink or ink text on blue (2.1).

Gradients are allowed only on blue: the arrow and beam fade from blue to light sky (#9DBDF5 → #E2ECFA), and stepped blocks fade from blue to deep blue (#0B2A7A). Never on lime.

Derived support tones (not in the deck): raised surface #16233F, muted text #A9B3C7 on dark / #4A5468 on light, hairline #2A3A5C, strong line #6E7C99.

## Typography

The typography page names three families. The deck's applications (strategy, elements, site and social slides) also use a heavier, wider grotesk for headlines. Until the designer confirms that face, this system uses **Archivo ExtraBold** (same family as Archivo Narrow) for it.

| Role | Face | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Statement | Archivo Narrow **Bold Italic**, uppercase | Covers, section openers, launch and statement pieces: A NEW WAY TO LOOK AT, REDES SOCIAIS, TIPOGRAFIA. The most recognisable type move — use it at least once per campaign. |
| Headline | Archivo ExtraBold (800), tight leading .9, tracking −2.5% | Web, social and email headlines. Uppercase for punchy lines (HARDWARE, SOFTWARE AND AI, SORTED.); sentence case for longer ones (One platform for a stronger, smarter business.). |
| Editorial accent | Instrument Serif **Italic** | One or two words inside a headline or a second line: "*importa?*", "*place.*", "*Real impact.*". Lower case or upper case. Never a whole paragraph. |
| Functional | DM Sans 400/500/600 | Body, navigation, forms, captions, eyebrow labels (600, +20% tracking, caps). |
| Tag | DM Mono 500, caps | The small tag on posts (NETWORKING, SORTED.), as in the deck's post mock-ups. Not on the typography page — confirm with the designer. |

Email falls back to Arial Black / Georgia / Arial / Courier New.

**The coloured full stop.** Headlines end in a round dot in an accent colour instead of a plain period: lime on ink or blue, blue on ivory, ink or blue on lime. The cover signs off *a!ert* with a blue dot. Size ≈ 19% of the cap height.

**Accent rule.** Colour the last phrase of a headline (lime on dark, blue on light), or switch it to Instrument Serif Italic. Not both in the same line.

## Logo

Lockup (a!ert + GLOBAL TECH), wordmark (a!ert) and monogram (a!), vectorised from the deck's master. The logo is only Ivory (on ink or blue) or Ink (on ivory or lime). Clear space = the height of the "a". Minimum width: lockup 120px, wordmark 64px, monogram 24px. Never recolour, stretch, outline the solid logo, add shadow, or put it on a photo without a solid block behind it. One logo per piece; wordmark top-left on posts.

**Outlined monogram pattern.** The a! drawn as a thin line (lime on ink, ink on ivory), enlarged and cropped by a circle or the frame edge. Use it as a supergraphic on banners, covers and profile variants — never as the identifying logo.

## Graphic elements

Simple, consistent, striking. Use two or three per piece, never all of them.
1. **Arrows** — the chunky up-right arrow (solid lime, blue or blue-to-sky gradient); line arrows; chevrons; arrow in a circle. One arrow per piece, large or bleeding off an edge.
2. **Lines** — hairline rails, vertical rules, crosshairs (+) in #6E7C99 or blue.
3. **Blocks** — solid blue and lime squares, outline squares, dashed squares, overlapping.
4. **Corners** — quarter-rounds and rounded corner lines.
5. **Basic shapes** — discs and squares, solid or outline.
6. **Patterns** — lime dot grid, diagonal hatching, fine blue grid. One per piece.
7. **Composition** — overlapping blocks, a disc over an outline ring, crosshair over a block corner.
8. **Numbering** — big Archivo numerals "01/04", small dots and dashes as bullets.
9. **Stepped blocks** — three ascending blocks (lime, blue, outline or blue gradient) as the growth motif from the social and case-study mock-ups.

**Header rail.** ALERT GLOBAL TECH ——— SECTION at left, 2026 —— at right, DM Sans 600 caps.

## Photography

Allowed and encouraged for commercial and brand-awareness posts (deck slide 17): architecture against blue sky, hardware product shots with a lime glow, real workplaces. Photos sit in a block or full-bleed behind solid type areas, never under body text. No stock handshakes, no faces as decoration. Customer photos only with permission.

## Inspiration (deck moodboard)

Editorial confidence over corporate safety: heavy grotesk next to italic serif, lime against cobalt, stacked and rotated type, crisp grids. Keep the energy; keep the B2B clarity.

## Email

600px, table-based, inline styles. Web fonts load only in some inboxes: Archivo → Arial Black; Instrument Serif → Georgia; DM Sans → Arial; DM Mono → Courier New.

Structure: hidden pre-header · Deep Ink header with the Ivory lockup · headline (Archivo ExtraBold caps, accent phrase in lime or Instrument Serif Italic) · stepped blocks as a divider · one idea in 60–120 words on ivory · one lime pill CTA (bulletproof, not an image) · Deep Ink footer.

Required in every marketing email (CAN-SPAM): postal address — 5185 S Commerce Dr, Murray, UT, United States — and a working unsubscribe link. Replies go to sales@alertglobaltech.com. Subject lines: sentence case, under 50 characters, no emoji, no exclamation marks.
